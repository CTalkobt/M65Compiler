; drums: a SID band with a sampled drum kit (doc/bin/tune45.md has the format)
;
; The SID voices show the instruments' newer parts: a bass through the
; low-pass filter, its cutoff sweeping up each note (a "wah"), with hard
; restart so every note starts alike; a lead whose wave table flips
; pulse and saw and an octave a frame (a buzz); arpeggio chords with a
; pulse-width sweep. The drums are digi voices (the audio DMA): kick and
; snare on one, the hi-hats on another, cut out of one CC0 kit
; (samples/SOURCE.txt) by from= and len=. tune45 makes them 8-bit: the kick
; and snare at the lowest rate that keeps them, the hats at 16 kHz (a
; hi-hat is all high frequencies: lower, there is nothing left of it).
;
; The form: the groove twice (A); the last bar speeds up (tempo:, a beat
; at a time) under a snare roll into the drive (B, 4.5 frames a tick: 167
; beats a minute): four on the floor, a pumping bass, a lead of repeated
; notes, the chords struck on every beat; twice, its last bar slowing to
; the groove again. Then round: A A B B. (The tune starts again at its own
; tempo: tune45 puts it at v1's start.)
